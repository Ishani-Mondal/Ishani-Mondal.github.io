Organizer · Microsoft Research India

A weekly lab-wide reading group on recent and classical work in speech and language processing, including arranging external speakers. Sustaining one for years is a teaching problem in its own right: choosing what a mixed-seniority room can productively read, and making it safe for the most junior person present to say they did not follow the paper.
